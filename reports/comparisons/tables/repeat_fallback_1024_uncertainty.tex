(1) Responses sampled with our PRC watermark show no statistically significant
differences from unwatermarked responses on any of the three diversity metrics.

than TextSeal, Gumbel-Max, or SynthID depth 30, even when these methods
use repeat handling. SynthID at depths 2 and 10
shows no statistically significant differences from our PRC watermark
on any of the three diversity metrics.
(3) Without repeat handling, responses sampled with SynthID depth 10,
TextSeal, or Gumbel-Max have significantly more repetition within each
response than those sampled with our PRC watermark.
Shared settings: Qwen3-8B-Base, temperature 1, full vocabulary;
50 shared prompts with two 1,024-token responses each;
target FPR $0.1\%$.
PRC watermark: online, $\eta=0.05$, $t=3$,
$r=0.99n$, posterior-mean detection.}

\footnotesize
\textit{Notes.} Self-BLEU measures average overlap between the two responses to each prompt.
Repeated 4-grams is the percentage of contiguous four-token sequences
occurring after their first appearance within a response.
Distinct-3 is the number of unique contiguous three-token sequences
divided by their total count. Both within-response metrics include
overlapping sequences and are averaged over 100 responses.
With repeat handling, unwatermarked sampling is used at positions where
the watermarking context repeats within a response. Without repeat
handling, the watermarking rule is still applied at those positions.
The two responses to each prompt use different sampling seeds;
each method's watermark keys are held fixed across responses.
Subscripts and superscripts give the lower and upper 95\% confidence
limits from 2,000 bootstrap resamples of prompts, keeping responses
and methods paired. Comparator estimates are bold for differences from PRC that remain
significant after Holm correction across all 35 two-sided paired
permutation tests ($\alpha=0.05$). Method labels are swapped within
prompts, keeping both responses together; tests use 100,000 permutations
or exact enumeration when feasible. The displayed confidence intervals
are unadjusted.
