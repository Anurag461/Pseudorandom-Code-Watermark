# PRC: tokens needed for 60% TPR at 10% token substitution

Qwen3-0.6B-Base, temperature 1, forced length, 500 prompts per length. PRC uses the
Table 15 setup at every length: fixed construction with n = T, t = 3, eta = 0.05, and
the posterior-mean detector with the analytical Hoeffding threshold. Substitution uses
the Table 15 attack (int(0.1 n) distinct positions, uniform replacement tokens).

Interpolated n is log-linear in n between the bracketing tested lengths; the bracket
is the defensible range. TPR in % with 95% Wilson intervals.

C4 prompts: 50-token RealNewsLike prefixes (`data/prompts.jsonl`). ELI5 prompts:
`Question: {q}\nAnswer:` over 500 seeded questions from `sentence-transformers/eli5`
(`data/eli5_prompts.jsonl`, provenance in `data/eli5_sources.json`).
